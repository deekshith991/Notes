# Transformer

## Keywords
1. N1 - No.of Turns on primary side
2. N2 - No.of Turns on secondary side
3. V1 - primary side voltage
4. V2 - secondary side voltage

## Terms
- $\vec{B}$: magnetic flux density (weber/m^2)
- $\vec{H}$: magnetic field Intensity (Amp/m)
- u: magnetic permeability (H/m)

## Formula
- $\vec{B}$ = u*$\vec{H}$

**Defination**: Transformer is a device that transfers the electrical energy from one electrical circuit to another electrical circuit through the medium of magnetic field and without a change of frequency while stepping up/down the voltage.

A static device with 99% Efficiency. Which can only work with AC voltage. Because for the transformer to work there needs to be change in the magnitude of current so that there will be change in the flux.

### Types of transformer
1. Step-up Transformer
2. Step-down Transformer
3. Unit Transformer

Parts of transformer
  1. Magnetic core
  2. Winding


## Step-up Transformer
This transformer the secondary sided winding has more No.of Turns than the primary side. So, the secondary side output voltage is more than the primary side voltage. Therefore the voltage is step-upped.

E.g: Generally used in the Generation side of the Power-system.

## Step-down Transformer
This transformer the secondary sided winding has less No.of Turns than the primary side. So, the secondary side output voltage is less than the primary side voltage. Therefore the voltage is step-down.

E.g: Generally used in the Distribution side of the Power-system.
